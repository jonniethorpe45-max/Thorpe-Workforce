# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 2026

## Introduction

Thorpe ("we", "our", "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how Thorpe handles your information.

## Data Storage

**All data is stored locally on your device.** Thorpe does not operate a central server that collects your personal information. Your data resides in a SQLite database in your operating system's application data directory.

### Data We Store Locally

| Data Type | Purpose | Retention |
|-----------|---------|-----------|
| Profile | Personalization | Until deleted |
| Scan history | Diagnostic reference | Until deleted |
| Diagnostic reports | Issue tracking | Until deleted |
| Repair history | Action audit log | Until deleted |
| Chat history | Conversation continuity | Until deleted |
| Settings | App preferences | Until deleted |
| License info | Feature access | Until deleted |

### Data We Do NOT Collect

- Passwords or credentials
- Browser history or bookmarks
- Personal documents or files
- Email content
- Financial information
- Location data
- Contacts or personal communications

## System Diagnostics

When you consent to a system scan, Thorpe collects technical information about your computer (OS version, hardware specs, running processes, disk usage, network configuration). This data:

- Is stored only on your device
- Is never transmitted without your explicit action
- Can be deleted at any time via Settings > Delete All User Data

## Cloud AI (Optional)

If you enable cloud AI in Settings:

- Your messages and system context may be sent to your configured AI provider (e.g., OpenAI)
- Your API key is stored locally and never sent to Thorpe servers
- You can disable cloud AI at any time and use local guidance instead
- Review your AI provider's privacy policy for their data handling

## Telemetry

Telemetry is **disabled by default**. Thorpe does not collect usage analytics unless you explicitly enable it.

## Data Sharing

Thorpe does not share your data with third parties. You control all data exports:

- PDF report exports are saved to locations you choose
- No automatic uploading or sharing occurs

## Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time:

1. Open **Settings**
2. Click **Delete All User Data**
3. Confirm twice

This permanently removes scans, reports, chat history, cases, clients, and settings.

## Children's Privacy

Thorpe is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be reflected in the application and this document.
