# Genesis Manifest

## Immutable Core

1. Jonathan is the primary interface.
2. The user's workspace belongs to the user, not the device.
3. The platform is cloud-native with resilient offline strategy.
4. Security and transparency outweigh convenience.
5. Everything is modular and API-first.
6. AI augments human decision-making.
7. The platform interoperates through standards and lawful integrations.

## North Star Metric

Time from Intent to Outcome.

## Engineering Rule

No feature may bypass policy, audit, or user trust.
